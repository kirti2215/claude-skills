# grill-me

Grill Kirti relentlessly about a plan, decision, or idea — a round of questions at a time — until there is shared understanding.

## When to use
- `/grill-me`
- "Grill me on this"
- "Stress test this idea"
- You want your thinking challenged before you commit to something

## Example prompts
- `/grill-me I'm thinking about pivoting my job search to focus only on AI strategy roles`
- `/grill-me here's the structure for my next article, tear it apart`
- `/grill-me I want to build a GTM skills library as an open-source repo`

## What it produces
Hard questions, one round at a time. No coaching. No affirmation. Just pressure until the idea either holds or breaks.
