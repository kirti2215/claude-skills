# gtm-regional-campaign-builder

Builds a full regional GTM campaign brief for any product, industry, and target market.

## When to use
- Planning a GTM campaign for a specific region
- "I need a campaign for X in Y" or "help me plan a launch in Europe"
- Regional positioning, campaign narrative, pipeline projections, or messaging for a specific geography
- Both external-facing campaigns and internal sales enablement

## Example prompts
- `/gtm-regional-campaign-builder [product] for APAC FSI customers`
- `/gtm-regional-campaign-builder EMEA enterprise campaign for [feature], focus on DACH and UK`

## What it produces
Full regional campaign brief: market context, messaging narrative, target segments, campaign channels, pipeline model, and sales enablement summary.
