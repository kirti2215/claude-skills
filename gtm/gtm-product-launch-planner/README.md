# gtm-product-launch-planner

Plans and builds a complete product launch package for any product, region, and audience.

## When to use
- "We're launching X in Y market"
- "Help me plan a launch"
- "I need launch messaging" or "build me a launch kit"
- Preparing personas, FAQs, objection handling, or internal launch materials

## Example prompts
- `/gtm-product-launch-planner launching [product] in EMEA for enterprise FSI customers`
- `/gtm-product-launch-planner build a launch kit for [feature] targeting mid-market`

## What it produces
Launch narrative, target personas, messaging hierarchy, FAQ, objection handling, and internal enablement materials.
