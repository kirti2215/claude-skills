# gtm-sales-enablement

Builds account-specific sales enablement materials for enterprise deals — pitch points, discovery questions, value props by persona, objection responses, and business case.

## When to use
- Preparing for an enterprise sales conversation
- Building account-specific materials for a specific deal
- "Help me prepare for a meeting with [company/persona]"
- Building a sales play for a specific vertical

## Example prompts
- `/gtm-sales-enablement I have a meeting with a CTO at a regional bank, we're selling [product]`
- `/gtm-sales-enablement build a sales play for FSI customers in APAC`
- `/gtm-sales-enablement discovery questions for a VP of Engineering at a Series B startup`

## What it produces
Account-specific: pitch narrative, discovery questions, value props by persona, top objections with responses, and a business case frame.
