# resume-quantifier

Ask Kirti discovery questions to help her recall real numbers behind her resume bullets.

## When to use
- You want to quantify experience but can't remember the numbers
- "Help me find metrics for this role"
- You're stuck on a bullet that feels vague but you know there's data somewhere

## Example prompts
- `/resume-quantifier [paste a vague bullet or role description]`
- `/resume-quantifier help me find numbers for my time at Databricks`

## What it produces
A series of targeted questions designed to jog your memory — team size, budget, pipeline influenced, % improvement, timeline, etc. Then helps you turn the answers into defensible metrics.
