# li-audit

Audit Kirti's published LinkedIn posts to find what actually worked and what to stop.

## When to use
- You paste post analytics or past posts
- You ask "what's working" or "why did this post flop"
- You want a pattern read across your content

## Example prompts
- `/li-audit [paste posts with engagement numbers]`
- `/li-audit here are my last 10 posts, what patterns do you see`

## What it produces
A breakdown of what drove engagement, what fell flat, and specific recommendations for what to do more or less of.
