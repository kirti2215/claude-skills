# li-human

Final AI-tell scan on a finished draft, with a five-check readout.

## When to use
- After running the humanizer skill
- When you ask "does this sound like AI?" or "will this get flagged?"
- As the last step before posting anything

## Example prompts
- `/li-human [paste your finished draft]`

## What it produces
A five-check readout flagging AI tells: unnatural phrasing, hedging patterns, structural giveaways, word choice, and flow. With specific fixes.
