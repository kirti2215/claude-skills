# li-dm

Adapted from Jake Schincariol's linkedin-agent-skill (MIT). The invite note is 200 characters. The first DM decides whether there is a second one. Neither is a pitch.

## Before writing, get the specifics

Ask in one batched question:

1. Who: name, role, company.
2. The hook: the actual reason to reach out now. A post they wrote, something their company shipped, a mutual connection, a talk they gave, shared history at AWS, Deloitte, Maersk or UNC.
3. What Kirti wants: a conversation, perspective, a referral, a role. Be honest internally even if the message does not lead with it.

If there is no specific reason to message this person now, say so.

## The invite note (200 characters)

One specific reference to them, one line about who Kirti is, and no ask. Count characters including spaces and show the count.

## The first message, after they accept

Wait a day. Then:

* Two to four sentences.
* Reference the specific thing from the note.
* Show she understands what their company or team does and where her experience fits. Make Kirti the star through concrete proof, not adjectives.
* One small ask, such as a 15 minute conversation.
* No calendar link in message one.

## Follow ups

Two, then stop.

* Day 4: add something new. Never "just bumping this". If there is nothing new, there is no follow up.
* Day 10: close the loop. Say she will stop, and mean it.

## Kirti's outreach rules

* Never mention visa sponsorship or work authorization.
* No shorthand or abbreviations: write product marketing and go to market, not PMM and GTM.
* No stock phrases such as "I'd welcome", "I hope this message finds you well", generic compliments, or exaggerated enthusiasm.
* For people she knows personally, reconnect and ask for perspective before asking for a referral.
* Never fabricate a mutual connection, shared school, or having read something she has not read.
* Never send automated sequences, and keep invites under 20 a day.

## Output

The invite note with its character count, the first message, and both follow ups with the day each goes out. Run everything through the humanizer skill. No dashes. Kirti sends each one by hand.
