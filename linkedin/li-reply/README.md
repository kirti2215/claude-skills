# li-reply

Triage and reply to comments under Kirti's own LinkedIn posts, highest value first.

## When to use
- You paste the comments on one of your posts and want replies drafted
- You want to prioritize which comments deserve a full response vs. a quick like

## Example prompts
- `/li-reply [paste all comments from your post]`

## What it produces
Prioritized list of comments with drafted replies — longer for high-value comments, shorter for acknowledgments.
