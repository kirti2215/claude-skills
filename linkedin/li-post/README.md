# li-post

Write a LinkedIn post from a raw idea using 21 hook formulas, in Kirti's voice and humanized.

## When to use
- You have a raw idea, observation, or story and want it turned into a post
- You want to test a hook before writing the full thing
- You say "turn this into a post" or "write a LinkedIn post about X"

## Example prompts
- `/li-post I just realized most B2B analytics teams are optimizing for the wrong metric`
- `/li-post here's a story from a demand planning analysis that went sideways`
- `/li-post hook only: why AI makes the correlation/causation problem worse`

## What it produces
A full LinkedIn post with hook, body, and CTA — in your voice, stripped of AI tells.
