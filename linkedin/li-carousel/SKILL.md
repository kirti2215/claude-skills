# li-carousel

Adapted from Jake Schincariol's linkedin-agent-skill (MIT). Document posts get high dwell time because each swipe counts. The format rewards one idea broken into steps and punishes a text post chopped into pieces.

## When to use it

Use a carousel when the idea has sequence: steps, a progression, a framework with parts (for example how she built an Agent Skill, or how a GTM motion is structured). If the idea is a single claim, say so and send her to li-post instead.

## Structure

8 to 12 slides.

1. Cover: the hook in six words or fewer, plus one line of promise.
2. The stake: why this matters, in one sentence.
3. One idea per slide: a headline of 3 to 7 words and at most 25 words under it. If a slide needs a paragraph, make it two slides.
4. Recap: the whole thing as a list, useful as a standalone screenshot.
5. Last slide: one action only.

## Copy rules

* The cover decides most of the result.
* Number every slide (3/10).
* No paragraphs on any slide.
* Her name small on every slide, since screenshots travel without her.
* Never invent numbers or outcomes; use only her verified work.

## Output

Slide by slide copy as a numbered list, then two or three lines of post text to sit above the carousel. Run both through the humanizer skill, with no dashes. Do not build a PDF unless Kirti explicitly asks for one; when she does, make it 1080 by 1350 per slide, one accent color, and text no smaller than 28px. She uploads it herself.
