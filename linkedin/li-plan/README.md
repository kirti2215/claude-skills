# li-plan

Plan Kirti's LinkedIn week: what to post, when, and who to engage with.

## When to use
- "Plan my week"
- "What should I post this week"
- You want a content calendar or engagement list

## Example prompts
- `/li-plan` (runs with context from memory)
- `/li-plan I want to focus on analytics and AI this week`
- `/li-plan I have an article dropping Thursday, build around that`

## What it produces
A weekly posting schedule with post ideas, optimal timing, and a list of people/posts to engage with.
