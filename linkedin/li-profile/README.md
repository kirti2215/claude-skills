# li-profile

Score Kirti's LinkedIn profile out of 100 on a 12-part rubric built for recruiter discovery, then rewrite the sections that lose the most points.

## When to use
- You ask to score, audit, or fix your LinkedIn profile
- You want to know what's costing you recruiter visibility

## Example prompts
- `/li-profile [paste your headline, about section, and experience]`
- `/li-profile score my profile`

## What it produces
A score out of 100 with a breakdown by section, plus rewrites of the weakest sections.
