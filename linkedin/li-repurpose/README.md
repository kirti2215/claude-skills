# li-repurpose

Turn one long asset (a Strategy Lens article, transcript, talk, or call summary) into four to six standalone LinkedIn posts.

## When to use
- You have a long piece of content (article, talk, interview) and want to squeeze more out of it
- You want posts that stand alone, not summaries

## Example prompts
- `/li-repurpose [paste article]`
- `/li-repurpose [link to Strategy Lens article]`

## What it produces
4–6 standalone LinkedIn posts, each hitting a different angle from the source material.
